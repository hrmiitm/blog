This is a place to keep notes on the web, useful tools, and things worth understanding.

Some posts are practical guides. Others start with a question or a small discovery. The aim is to leave enough detail and sources that you can try things yourself.

## Keep in touch

Follow the [RSS feed](../index.xml) to get new posts in your reader. You can also find the writing and site source on [GitHub](https://github.com/hrmiitm/blog).

## Corrections are welcome

Tools change, pages move, and details can be wrong. If you spot something that needs updating, [open an issue](https://github.com/hrmiitm/blog/issues) with the post link and the correction.
