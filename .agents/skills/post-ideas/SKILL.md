---
name: post-ideas
description: Develop and choose blog topics, useful questions, and distinctive article angles before drafting.
---

Read existing posts to avoid repetition and understand the notebook's scope. Establish what the reader should learn or be able to do.

Generate a small set of distinct angles, including practical guides, comparisons, experiments, and explanations when relevant. Prefer an answerable question or a concrete task over a broad topic. Identify what evidence each promising angle needs. Do not invent a personal experience to provide a hook.

Recommend the strongest angle and a working title, with the intended reader and the evidence needed to proceed. Keep alternatives brief. An experiment proposal is not a result; leave the result open until tested.
