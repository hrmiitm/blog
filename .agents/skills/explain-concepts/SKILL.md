---
name: explain-concepts
description: Make unfamiliar or complex concepts clear in tutorials and explanatory blog passages.
---

Find the specific obstacle: invisible mechanism, unfamiliar scale, too many moving parts, or missing background. Start from a concrete example the intended reader can follow, then introduce the formal idea.

Use a comparison, a short sequence, or a diagram only when it maps to the actual mechanism. State where an analogy stops working. Do not let a sequence imply causation without evidence.

Introduce unfamiliar terms when needed and define them near their first use. Group related details and reveal complexity gradually. For a tutorial, make prerequisites, steps, expected results, and common failure cases explicit. Verify commands or examples when practical; distinguish tested steps from documentation-based guidance.

End with what the reader can now understand or do. Do not add an interactive widget when a short explanation is sufficient.
