---
name: data-story
description: Write an evidence-based blog article from a dataset, benchmark, or experiment without overstating its results.
---

Establish the data source, question, units, time range, missing values, and meaningful comparison. Keep analysis reproducible in a small script when useful; record transformations and exclusions.

Check promising findings against alternative explanations, sample selection, uncertainty, and reasonable changes in assumptions. Distinguish correlation from causation. A surprising observation is a lead, not proof.

Choose the article structure for the reader: finding-first for a quick comparison, discovery sequence for a longer investigation, or step-by-step for teaching. Support each major claim with a concrete case or chart. Keep important limitations next to the finding they affect.

Use `post-visuals` for charts. Publish the source data or method when allowed and useful. Do not fabricate benchmarks, measurements, or test outcomes to complete a narrative.
